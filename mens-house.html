Men's House

Location & Capacity

Our men's sober living home is located in Canton, Ohio and accommodates up to 10 residents.

Amenities

  • Private and shared bedroom options
  • Fully equipped kitchen
  • Common living areas
  • Laundry facilities on-site
  • Parking available
  • 24/7 peer support

House Guidelines

  • Zero tolerance for drugs and alcohol
  • Regular drug testing
  • Mandatory house meetings
  • Participation in recovery activities encouraged
  • Mutual respect and accountability
