Women's House

Location & Capacity

Our women's sober living home is located in Massillon, Ohio, and accommodates up to 12 residents.

Amenities

  • Private and shared bedroom options
  • Fully equipped kitchen
  • Comfortable common areas
  • Laundry facilities on-site
  • Parking available
  • 24/7 peer support
  • Safe and supportive environment

House Guidelines

  • Zero tolerance for drugs and alcohol
  • Regular drug testing
  • Mandatory house meetings
  • Participation in recovery activities encouraged
  • Community and sisterhood focus
