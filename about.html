About Us

Our Mission

Cusma Sober Housing is dedicated to providing safe, supportive, and affordable sober living environments for individuals in recovery. We believe that stable housing is a crucial component of long-term sobriety and personal growth.

Our Approach

We create structured, peer-supported environments where residents can focus on their recovery while building life skills and developing healthy relationships. Our homes emphasize accountability, mutual support, and personal responsibility.

What We Offer

  • Structured sober living environment
  • Peer support and accountability
  • Connection to recovery resources
  • Life skills development
  • Community and fellowship
  • Affordable housing options

Our Team

Our team consists of dedicated individuals who are passionate about supporting those in recovery. Many of our staff members are in recovery themselves and understand the challenges and triumphs of the journey.
