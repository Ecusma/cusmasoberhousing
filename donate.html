Help Keep Sober Living Affordable and Available

Your generous donations help us maintain affordable housing options for individuals working on their recovery. Every contribution makes a difference in someone's journey to sobriety.

How Your Donation Helps

  • Subsidize rent for residents in financial need
  • Maintain and improve our facilities
  • Provide recovery resources and materials
  • Support community activities and programs
  • Ensure 24/7 availability of support
  • Expand our capacity to serve more individuals

Ways to Give

We accept donations of all sizes. Whether it's a one-time gift or a recurring contribution, your support helps us continue our mission of providing safe, supportive housing for those in recovery.

Other Ways to Support

  • Volunteer your time
  • Donate household items
  • Spread awareness about our mission
  • Become a community partner
